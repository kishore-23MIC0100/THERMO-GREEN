# Reproducible records

Record an objective, operator/setup notes, sensor placement, initial temperature, reagent/media description and workload profile. Keep real and simulated runs distinct. CSV source values are `live` or `simulated`; replay does not rewrite source. Simulation speed is recorded at session creation.

Thermal analysis: Q = mass × Cp × (last−first reactor temperature). Defaults assume 330 g water and Cp=4.184 J/g·°C, with no explicit correction for block thermal mass or losses. The 40 W nameplate gives an ideal upper-bound heating rate near 1.7 °C/min and ideal 24→35.5 °C heating time near 6.5 minutes. These are estimates, not measured recovered server energy.

Heating rate uses a linear fit over the last 60 seconds of recorded wall time. Duty integrates time intervals, excluding telemetry gaps greater than two seconds. Maximum temperature and trip counts come from stored samples. Linear time-to-target estimates are absent when slope is nonpositive. Do not interpret accelerated simulator rate as physical rate.

Chemical comparison: use matched lime-water concentration/volume, vessel, gas supply, mixing and exposure duration. Run an ambient control and heated condition. Record precipitation, filter/dry consistently, subtract an appropriate blank, identify the solid and document balance calibration. Enter dry mass as MEASURED / USER-ENTERED. The UI intentionally does not convert an unverified precipitate into a carbon-removal claim.

Biological research requires actual OD680 or dry-weight biomass, pH, culture temperature, light, inlet/outlet CO₂ and a carbon mass balance. Approximate 30–35 °C Spirulina optimum and 38–40 °C damage ranges depend on culture conditions. No growth data is generated by this application.
